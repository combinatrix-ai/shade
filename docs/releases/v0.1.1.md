Shade 0.1.1 makes unattended MacBook sessions more practical and gives the menu panel clearer control over when and why the display dims.

## Highlights

- Keep the Mac awake in **Clamshell mode** when its lid is closed on power adapter, even with no online display target.
- Dim every online display together. Shade uses native brightness where available and gamma-table dimming for external, software, and virtual displays that do not expose it.
- Choose any whole-minute dimming delay from 1 to 60 minutes, with quick presets and an inline countdown editor.
- Use the new **Only on power adapter** policy. Shade restores and pauses when power is disconnected, then resumes with a fresh countdown when power returns.
- See the effective Auto-Lock state directly in the panel, including configured inactivity intervals and prevention by Shade or another app.
- More reliably ignore the physical gesture that initiated dimming before Wake on touch becomes active.

Clamshell mode with no online displays controls sleep prevention only. When an external or virtual display appears, Shade starts a fresh countdown and includes it in the next dim. Lid-closed operation requires AC power. Shade remains an Apple Silicon app for macOS 14 or later.

A dark display is still an unlocked Mac. Shade is not a security boundary or a substitute for locking sensitive work.
