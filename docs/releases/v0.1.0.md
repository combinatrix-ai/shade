Shade is a temporary privacy band-aid for Computer Use: darken the built-in display while the desktop stays available to your agent.

## Highlights

- Dim automatically after physical inactivity and dim again after restoring the display.
- Restore with a customizable shortcut, brightness-up key, or optional physical input detection.
- Compact menu-bar controls, in-panel settings, and a first-launch recovery tutorial.
- Signed and notarized Apple Silicon app with Sparkle updates and optional launch at login.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. Download Shade.dmg, drag Shade to Applications, and launch it. Shade starts off.

A dark display is still an unlocked Mac. Shade is not a security boundary or a substitute for locking sensitive work. Physical-input detection is best effort; Wake on touch also responds to a physical mouse or keyboard and does not detect a stationary finger alone. Idle-lock prevention can vary with system or managed policy.

This is the first public release. An update from a prior public version cannot yet be tested; verify actual version-to-version replacement at the next release.
